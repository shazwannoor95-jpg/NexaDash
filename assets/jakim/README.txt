Annual official JAKIM e-Solat schedules, downloaded over verified HTTPS.
Source, retrieval time and file hashes are recorded in source.json.
These are official zone schedules, not locally calculated prayer times.
Refresh all four using: python3 tools/update_jakim_cache.py
Coverage is limited to the dates actually present; expired days must not be reused.
The app can refresh annual data online without replacing the APK.
